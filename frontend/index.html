Enter the details below to estimate the house price:

What do these inputs mean?

  • MedInc: Average income in the area. Higher value = richer neighborhood.
  • HouseAge: How old the houses are (in years).
  • AveRooms: Average number of rooms per house.
  • AveBedrms: Average number of bedrooms per house.
  • Population: Number of people living in that area.
  • AveOccup: Average number of people living in one house.
  • Latitude & Longitude: Location of the area (map coordinates).

Important Notes

  • All values should be positive numbers.
  • Latitude should be between 32 and 42.
  • Longitude should be between -125 and -114.
  • The prediction is an estimate, not an exact price.
  • Prices are calculated based on patterns in California housing data.
